Occlusion vs Grad-CAM Analysis
==============================

- Do both methods highlight similar regions?
Yes, they both generally point to the river, but not in the exact same way or shape.

- Which explanation appears more localized?
The occlusion sensitivity map. Grad-CAM tends to create larger, blurry blobs, while occlusion marks more exact and bounded patches.

- Which explanation appears easier to interpret?
Visually, Grad-CAM is more intuitive and quicker to read at a glance because it's smoother. However, the logic behind occlusion ("if I cover this, the model fails") is much easier to justify.

- Did both methods identify the river region?
Yes, both methods ignored the background vegetation and focused on the actual water to make the decision.

- What happens for unknown inputs?
The results lose their meaning. Grad-CAM tries to force heatmaps onto background noise (like with the highway), and occlusion gives random maps because hiding a patch of an image the model already doesn't understand doesn't cause logical confidence drops.

- Can one explanation method completely replace another?
No. As the theory says, they answer different questions. Grad-CAM tells you "where the model is looking," and Occlusion tells you "what parts are strictly necessary so it doesn't change its mind." They complement each other perfectly.
