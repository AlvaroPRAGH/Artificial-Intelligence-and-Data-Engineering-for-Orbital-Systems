CAM Methods Comparison
======================

- Do all CAM methods highlight the same image regions?
No. They usually agree on the main subject (like the river), but the exact shapes, intensity, and spread of the heatmaps are quite different across methods.

- Which method produces the sharpest visualization?
LayerCAM produces the sharpest and most detailed heatmaps, capturing finer edges and smaller features.

- Which method produces the smoothest visualization?
GradCAM is definitely the smoothest and most blurred out of the four.

- Which method appears easiest to interpret?
GradCAM is the easiest for a quick general glance, but LayerCAM is better if you want to understand exactly which specific pixels influenced the decision.

- Do any methods produce suspicious or unexpected attention?
Yes, sometimes methods like EigenCAM highlight random background textures or edges that don't seem related to the actual class we are looking for.

- What does this suggest about relying on only one XAI method?
It proves that relying on just one method is risky. Each algorithm calculates attention differently and has its own blind spots. Using multiple methods gives a much more honest and complete view of the model's real behavior.
